Brief: take your notes out of Jotter approved

People who write in Jotter cannot get their notes out in a form other tools read. This brief states the problem, who has it, and what "fixed" looks like.

The problem

A user kept a year of trip plans in Jotter. She moved to a new laptop and wanted the plans in the text editor she uses for everything else. She opened each note, selected all, and pasted it. Forty notes later, every checklist had become plain lines, and two links had lost their addresses.

Jotter has no way to take notes out. Copy and paste works for one note, drops structure, and does not scale to a whole account. People who want a copy of their own writing, as a backup or to move tools, have no good path.

Who it affects

  • Long-time writers with dozens of notes who want a backup they control.
  • People moving tools who need their notes in a format other editors open.
  • People sharing one note with someone who does not use Jotter.

Today and wanted

Today

Open a note, select all, copy, paste somewhere else. Repeat per note. Checklists become plain text. Links keep their words but can lose their address.

Wanted

One action saves a note as a plain file. One action saves every note. The files open in any text editor and read the same as they do in Jotter.

Success

Each criterion is something a user can do afterwards.

SC-1 A user can save any single note as a file that opens in a plain text editor, with its title, headings, checklists (and their ticked state) and links intact.
SC-2 A user can save every note in their account in one action, and gets one file per note.
SC-3 Saved file names are safe on every common desktop system and never collide, even when two notes share a title or a title has characters such as "/" or "?".

Appetite

One pull request, about a day of work. If it needs more than that, we cut to single-note export first.

Constraints

  • No new third-party service. Files come straight from Jotter.
  • Nothing about how notes are stored changes. See the note body explainer.
  • Only the signed-in owner can export their notes.

Non-goals

  • Importing Markdown back into Jotter.
  • Other formats such as PDF or HTML.
  • Exporting attachments or images. Jotter notes have none today.
  • Scheduled or automatic backups.
