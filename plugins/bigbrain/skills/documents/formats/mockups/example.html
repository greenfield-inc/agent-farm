Mockups: dark-mode toggle built

Two rounds for the Appearance row in Settings. Round 2 option B was approved and built.

Back to the brief · Design notes

Brief

Screen: Settings, signed in. What changes: a new Appearance row with a theme choice, and dark values for the whole screen. What must be preserved: the existing rows, their order, the card layout, and the 480px breakpoint from the design notes.

Current

Rounds

Round 1

Option A: one switch · settings-v1-a.svg

Approach A "Dark mode" on/off switch. Self-critique Simplest, but it cannot express "follow my device", and off is ambiguous. Invented details the label text.

Option B: segmented control · settings-v1-b.svg

Approach System, Light and Dark side by side. Self-critique All choices visible; needs a plan for narrow screens, and selection shown by colour only. Invented details System as the default.

Option C: dropdown · settings-v1-c.svg

Approach A dropdown. Self-critique Compact, but hides the choices and needs two taps. Invented details none.

Round 2

Option B, refined · settings-v2-b.html approved

Approach Round 1's B with a check mark on the selected segment, a hint line, a full-width layout under 480px, and a focus ring that differs from the accent in dark. Self-critique The hint adds a line to every theme row; worth it for "System". Invented details the hint wording.

Approved

Round 2 option B, settings-v2-b.html, approved via Q-1 → D-1. Rebuilt in HTML so the verifier can compare it exactly. Status: built.
