Layout

  • One centred column, max width 720px, 16px gutters on phones.
  • One breakpoint at 480px: below it, a row's control drops under its label and takes the full width.
  • Cards have a 10px radius and a 1px border; rows inside a card are split by 1px lines.

Components

  • Settings row: src/settings/SettingsRow.tsx. Label, value or control, optional hint.
  • Button group: src/ui/ButtonGroup.tsx. Reuse it for a segmented control; it already handles arrow keys.
  • Card: src/ui/Card.tsx.

Density and tone

Calm and sparse: one card, a handful of rows, lots of space. Copy is short and plain: labels are nouns ("Appearance"), hints are one sentence in the second person.
