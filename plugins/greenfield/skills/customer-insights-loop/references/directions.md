# Studies and the directions page

## A study report

One page per study question:

1. **Headline:** the one finding that most changes what gets built.
2. **The question for the person:** the decision this study puts to them, with a recommended answer.
3. **Findings,** numbered, each with its number from the query set and a link to that query.
4. **Recommendations,** ranked, each naming the findings it rests on.
5. **Method:** sources, queries, filters, and what the data cannot show.

## A competitor note

One page per competitor: what it offers for each study question, read from a copy of its public site, docs or product, with a source cited in every cell. Mark what you could not verify.

## The directions page

One page, in this order:

1. **At a glance:** a table of every direction with its rank, a one-line problem, its evidence, effort and market labels.
2. **Competitor matrix:** directions down the side, competitors across, each cell what that competitor offers, with its source.
3. **One card per direction,** in rank order:
   - labels: evidence (strong, moderate, thin), effort (small, medium, large), market (gap, parity, ahead);
   - the problem, with its numbers linked to their queries;
   - the proposal;
   - the mockup, Today beside Proposed, marked illustrative;
   - a success metric and the events that measure it, naming any event that does not exist yet;
   - risks and open questions.
4. **Recommended order,** and why.
5. **Decisions for the person,** each with a recommended answer. These are the agenda of the picks sitting.
