Checked and left out on purpose

  • IndexNow as a route to Google. Google does not read it. The 41 October pages were submitted through it and most stayed unindexed; links decide indexing (item 2).
  • More definition or glossary pages. Explainers run 0.87%, and 1 of 14 October definition pages was indexed. Existing ones are judged by AI views; no new ones.
  • Compare pages for every newly found tool. 2 of 9 new compare pages were indexed. Alternatives pages for competitors buyers choose between come first (workstream 1).
  • New locales. Spanish and German leak; they are diagnosed first (item 18).
  • Moving compare pages under one URL scheme. Those URLs earn traffic, and a move resets indexing.
  • Another title round on pages the results page already answers. Earlier rounds did not move click rate; item 20 checks first.

Limits of this run

  • No sign-up survey was available to this run, so the AI share of sign-ups is unknown; tracking alone undercounts it.
  • Search Console's Generative AI report was not transcribed (the API does not expose it), so AI-feature views of the explainers are not on this page.
  • Analytics has a hole from Sep 9 to Oct 5. Every reading that spans it says so.
  • The citation probe has not run yet (item 3); AI findings come from referrers only.
  • is-agentic returned a cached scan dated 2026-08-31; Ora's scan is from 2026-11-03.
  • Live checks ran once, on 2026-11-04, from outside the site.
