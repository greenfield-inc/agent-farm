Booking and checkout guide

This is fictional documentation for the Workshop Atlas template example, not evidence about a real product.

  1. Choose the tool and desired reservation slot.
  2. Assign the reservation to the member.
  3. At checkout, record the member holding the tool.
  4. When a tool is returned, close its checkout entry.
  5. Share this guide with volunteers at the desk.
  6. Review active reservations and checkouts during the next volunteer handover.
